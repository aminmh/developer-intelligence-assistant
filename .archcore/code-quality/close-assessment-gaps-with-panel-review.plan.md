## Goal

Make one Code Quality assessment find what a ten-reviewer AI panel found on skiplanner-api (Symfony 6.3, commit `09c177555d9bcd40a9ea490dc44abb2ad39fac75`, panel report of 2026-11-01), across the same ten categories, at a fraction of its cost (about $98.6) and with every citation verified.

Baseline (measured 2026-11-16 on that commit, offline analyzers in the `api-python-api` image): inventory 1,822 files (1,817 PHP); 17 built-in Semgrep rules gave 31 findings in 5 s (20 `SELECT *`, 6 empty catch, 2 dynamic execution, 2 global state, 1 credential); deterministic Clean Code metrics covered 1,812 units in 5 s and matched the panel's maintainability examples exactly (`SupportCategoryEnum::schema` 489 lines, cyclomatic 44; `Tenant\ClientController` 645 lines, LCOM4 11, fan-out 24), but sent only 18 of about 780 threshold-exceeding candidates to the model. Of the panel's decisive Security, Reliability and Performance findings, the analyzers hit one, and only generically (`SportClubRemoverService.php` line 71 as dynamic execution). The model review sees about 12 KB of retrieved code. Five panel categories have no category here, and their evidence files are not inventoried.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The report scores ten categories; the weighted overall score exists only when all ten are assessed.
- On skiplanner `09c17755`, the deterministic stages alone find the public support route, the unprotected tenant routes, the `rm -rf` after `setDomain(null)` and the PHPUnit version conflict. The unbounded `itemsPerPage` and the Sentry sample rate are expected from the review loop instead (user decision 2026-11-16, following tasks 9 and 11).
- With the review loop, at least 35% of the gold set's decisive findings appear at the panel's location (40% counting one-call-hop related locations), each with a verified citation; 0 accepted findings cite a range no tool returned. (Lowered from 70% by the user on 2026-11-21; see Clarifications.)
- Every category score lies within one point (on the panel's 0–10 scale) of the panel's final, or the report explains the difference with evidence.
- One full assessment costs under 10% of the panel's $98.6 and finishes within 15 minutes on that commit.
- No stage executes project code, tests, builds or package scripts.
